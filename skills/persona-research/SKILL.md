Act as a UX Researcher and Product Strategist.
Before we start, if there is no product idea in this chat, ask for:

1. What is my **product idea**?
2. What **country or region** am I targeting?
3. Who are my **target users** (if known)? (e.g., students, freelancers, parents, etc.)

If there is already a product idea in this chat, proceed ahead.

Once I provide these details, follow the steps below:

---

### Step 1: Persona Overview

Create a realistic and detailed persona profile that includes:

**Name:** (fictional but relatable)

**Age Range:**

**Occupation / Role:**

**Location:**

**Income Range:**

**Tech Familiarity:** (low / medium / high)

**Lifestyle Snapshot:** 2–3 sentences describing their daily life and priorities.

---

### Step 2: Goals and Motivations

- What does this person want to achieve in their life or work?
- What motivates them to use this product?
- What outcomes or benefits are they seeking?
- Which emotional or social needs drive their behaviour?

---

### Step 3: Pain Points and Challenges

- What frustrations or barriers does this persona face related to this problem?
- What tools, methods, or workarounds do they currently use?
- What’s stopping them from reaching their goal today?

---

### Step 4: Behaviours and Attitudes

- How do they discover, evaluate, and adopt new products or technologies?
- What are their habits (daily or weekly)?
- How do they make decisions (logical, emotional, peer-influenced)?
- How do they measure success or satisfaction?

---

### Step 5: Relationship with the Product

- Describe how this persona would use your product — when, where, and why.
- What feature(s) or experience(s) would matter most to them?
- How might they talk about your product to others?

---

---

### Final Output

Conclude with a 2–3 sentence narrative summary:

> “This persona represents [target segment], who wants to [core motivation].
> 
> 
> They struggle with [key problem], and value products that [desired experience or outcome].”
